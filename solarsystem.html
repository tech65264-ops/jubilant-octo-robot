Solar System

The Solar System consists of the Sun and all the objects that orbit around it.

The Sun is at the centre of our Solar System. It is a star that provides heat and light to Earth.

There are eight planets in our Solar System. They are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune.

The planets revolve around the Sun in different orbits.

Our Solar System also contains moons, asteroids, comets and dwarf planets.

Jupiter is the largest planet, while Mercury is the smallest planet.

The Solar System is part of the Milky Way Galaxy.
