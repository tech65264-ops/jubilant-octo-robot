Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet that supports life. It is often called the Blue Planet because most of its surface is covered with water.

Earth has seven continents and five oceans. It has land, water, air, mountains, forests and many different types of animals and plants.

Earth takes about 365 days to complete one revolution around the Sun. It takes 24 hours to complete one rotation on its axis.

The Moon is Earth's natural satellite.

We must protect our planet by keeping it clean and reducing pollution.
