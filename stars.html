Stars

Stars are huge balls of hot gases that produce their own light and heat.

The Sun is the closest star to Earth and is the centre of our Solar System.

Stars appear small in the night sky because they are very far away from Earth.

Stars come in different colours, sizes and temperatures.

Some stars are red, yellow, white and blue.

Stars are born in huge clouds of gas and dust called nebulae.

They shine brightly in the night sky and make our universe beautiful.

The Milky Way is home to billions of stars.
