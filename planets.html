Planets

There are eight planets in our Solar System. Each planet is unique and has its own special features.

1. Mercury is the smallest planet and closest to the Sun.

2. Venus is the hottest planet in our Solar System.

3. Earth is our home planet and supports life.

4. Mars is known as the Red Planet.

5. Jupiter is the largest planet in our Solar System.

6. Saturn is famous for its beautiful rings.

7. Uranus is an ice giant that rotates on its side.

8. Neptune is the farthest planet from the Sun.

All eight planets revolve around the Sun in their own orbits
