Galaxies

A galaxy is a huge collection of stars, planets, gas, dust and other objects held together by gravity.

There are billions of galaxies in the universe.

Our Solar System is located in a galaxy called the Milky Way.

Galaxies come in different shapes and sizes.

There are three main types of galaxies: spiral, elliptical and irregular.

The Milky Way is a spiral galaxy.

Some galaxies contain billions or even trillions of stars.

Galaxies are very beautiful and mysterious parts of our universe.

Scientists use powerful telescopes to study galaxies and learn more about space.
